MONTHLY SALES TREND 

Task: Summarize sales by month and visualize the trend.
Objective: Practice date grouping and line charts.
Dataset: Sample - Superstore (9,994 orders, Jan 2014 - Dec 2017)
Tool used: Excel

FILE
Monthly_Sales_Trend.xlsx

WHAT IS INSIDE

Sheet “Monthly Sales”
Monthly table: Month and Total Sales ($) for all 48 months, sorted chronologically
Grand total: $2,297,200.86 (matches the total of the Sales column in the CSV)
Line chart: Monthly Sales Trend (Month on X-axis, Sales on Y-axis)
Sheet “Data”
Order Date and Sales columns copied from the Superstore CSV

STEPS FOLLOWED

Loaded the Superstore CSV.
Converted Order Date from text (M/D/YYYY) to proper dates.
Grouped Sales by month using SUMIFS formulas.
Kept months in chronological order.
Created a line chart from the monthly table.
Recalculated and checked: no formula errors, and the total matches the source data.

KEY OBSERVATION
Sales trend upward over the four years, with peaks around November and December each year.
